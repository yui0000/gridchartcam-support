Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Information We Collect

SimpleGridCam does not collect any personal information. Camera access is used only to display a grid and level guide overlay on the live preview, and captured photos are saved directly to your device's photo library. No data is transmitted to any external server.

Camera and Photo Library

SimpleGridCam uses your device's camera to show a grid overlay for framing your shots, and requests photo library access only to save the photos you capture. These photos remain on your device and are never uploaded or shared with third parties.

Third-Party Services

SimpleGridCam uses Apple's StoreKit for in-app purchases. Apple's privacy policy applies to any purchase transactions.
